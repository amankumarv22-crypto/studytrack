About StudyTrack

StudyTrack is a student academic planner. It tracks assignments and deadlines, records assessment marks, shows subject-wise performance, and keeps a study streak and marks goals.

Built with: Flask, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL (SQLite locally), Chart.js, a Progressive Web App layer, and an Android WebView app.

Developed by: Aman Verma
